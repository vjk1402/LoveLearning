Love Note 3

Context Window

The context window is how much of your conversation the AI can hold in its heart at one moment — its short-term memory, its little writing desk.

💌 The real-world example

Picture writing a long love letter on a small desk. Only so many pages fit on the desk at once. As you add new pages at the bottom, the earliest pages slide off the edge and out of sight.

That desk is the context window. The AI can only “see” what fits on it right now. Say something far beyond that edge, and it simply won't remember — not because it doesn't care, but because that page already slipped off the table.

🔑 The core idea

  • The window is measured in tokens (remember Note 2 — the puzzle pieces).
  • It counts everything: your messages, the AI's replies, and any documents you paste in.
  • When the conversation gets too long, the oldest parts fall out of memory first.

A “128K context” model can keep about 128,000 tokens (~96,000 words) on the desk at once.

💡 Why it matters for us

If the AI ever “forgets” something you said much earlier, it's usually the context window, not a flaw in the model. Keeping important details close — or re-sharing them — keeps them on the desk. This is also the exact problem that RAG (Chapter Two 💕) was invented to solve.
