Tokens

AI doesn't read whole words the way we do. It breaks text into small puzzle pieces called tokens — and everything it charges, remembers and processes is counted in tokens.

🧩 The real-world example

Think of building a word out of LEGO bricks. The word “unbelievable” isn't one brick — it's snapped together from smaller pieces:

un · believ · able

An LLM does the same. It chops text into tokens (whole words, parts of words, punctuation) and works with those bricks. Roughly, 1 token ≈ 4 characters ≈ ¾ of a word in English.

🔑 Why you should care

  • Cost: AI APIs charge per token — both what you send and what it replies.
  • Limits: Every model can only handle so many tokens at once (that's tomorrow's topic — the context window).
  • Speed: More tokens = more to process = slower and pricier.

💡 Quick rule of thumb

A short paragraph is ~100 tokens. This whole page is a few hundred. When someone says “a 128K context model”, they mean it can hold about 128,000 tokens (≈ 96,000 words) in mind at once.
